| B1 | High above our planet, a laboratory the size of a football field circles Earth every ninety minutes. That is the International Space Station, flying two hundred and fifty miles above the surface at seventeen thousand five hundred miles per hour. Sixteen times each day, the station laps the planet, and from its cupola, Earth looks like a living painting. The crew watches continents drift by, oceans swell, and clouds roll across the globe — a constant, turning view of the world we all share. From this height there are no borders drawn on the surface, only land and ocean and ice. [claim:ISS-001] | ISS-001 | SINGLE |

This is Earth observation from orbit, and it helps scientists understand our changing planet. The cupola has supported robotics operations, docking, and photography for more than a decade.
